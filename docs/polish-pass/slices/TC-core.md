## What was weak

Of the 62 live tier 7+ core cards, 27 played the shared `GodEvent` arc in `sigVisuals.tsx`: a whole-board wash, a five-ray god fan, a colossal figure lowered or raised over the middle of the board, a flare, a spark burst and two shock rings. The figure was card-unique, but it said nothing about the rule, and the other five layers were the same on all 27 cards whatever they did. Those 27 are exactly the cards in this pass: all live tier 7 and above cards on `GodEvent` are now off it (the 26 `GodEvent` uses left are tier 6 and below or retired cards).

## How the new scenes work

Each lead now draws its rule: the ranks, squares and pieces the card touches, with turn counts as pips, and small piece glyphs as a rule chart when the card names piece types (what is frozen and what is free, what is spared). The helpers sit above the apex band in `sigVisuals.tsx`: `dm` (stagger plus a --fx-dur scaled beat), `rankTop` and `bandTop` (the caster's ranks in `BoardFrame`, right for either player through `--fx-side`), `tcCellBox` (a square's box in the cast-anchored canvas), `TcGlyph` (ApexPiece plus a king) and `TcHourglass`. The verbs are the `fx-tc-*` classes at the end of `effects.css` (drop, slam, hold, pip, wipex, wipey, strike, sweep, hop, glance, crumble, glint, flip, ward, void, pull, rise, topple, spin, arrive): transform and opacity only, `calc(duration * var(--fx-dur, 1))`, `--ease-*` tokens, one-shot, and parked hidden under `html[data-anim="off"]` by one attribute rule. Colours are each card's existing three-colour palette from its old art. No shadow, glow, filter or blur. Target cuts (the per-square hits) and the entrance route are unchanged.

## Where I stopped

Stopped at a clean commit (0d01afa) after 27 cards: every live tier 7 and above core card that was on the shared `GodEvent` arc. The 35 other live tier 7+ core cards already had their own `BoardWideStage` scenes and were not re-audited this pass; next in line, by tier, are the ones whose leads are mostly a wash plus one motif and not the rule: the tier 10 and 9 apex set (total_war, oblivion, ascendancy, grand_army, eternal_freeze, queens_apocalypse, full_resurrection, ice_age, mass_petrify, living_god, resurrection, iron_legion, second_coming, blackout, philosophers_stone), then the tier 8 non-GodEvent set (titan, divine_fortress, heavens_wrath, berserker, cataclysmic_meteor, boneca_ambalabu, divine_right, i_love_my_gf), then tier 7 (queens_rampage, god_knight, triple_amazon, mass_freeze, deep_freeze, dragons_breath, celestial_ascension, roost_of_rocs, roulette, brr_brr_patapim, golden_touch, magnetism, termites). A contact sheet (`--module core --tier 8,7 --sheet`) would rank them; it was not run to keep the heavy slots free.
