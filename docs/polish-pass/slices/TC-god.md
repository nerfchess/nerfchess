## Where I stopped

Round 2 stopped at a clean commit after batch 7 (32 cards this round). No card in godPlays is on a shared template any more except peace_of_the_grave (T9, still blocked: its rule names the squares next to the caster's KING and the play only knows the cast square; see REQUESTS). culling (SkullStrike) and grand_conjunction (PlanetAlign) are one-card apex set pieces already built for their own card and were left alone. The templates GodDescent, TitanRise, SkyWrath, AbyssMaw, HostMarch, CelestialRing, FrostTitan, ForgeColossus, GorgonIdol and ChronoLord now back no card; they are dead code in the lazy chunk and can be deleted in a follow-up (lint does not flag unused function declarations here). Next for this slice: that deletion, then peace_of_the_grave once Board passes the king square.

Round 1 strips for chisel_curse, cockatrice_gaze, molten_heart and withered_hands were captured from the batch-1 commit. In round 2 the first before run for the first six cards accidentally caught the new scenes (the dev server had hot-reloaded); they were re-captured with HEAD's godPlays.tsx and .css swapped in for the run and restored after.

Round 2 notes:
- Several scenes read the victim order through `--fx-index` in a static `opacity: clamp(...)` wrapper (queen_storm's queen or rook, statue_garden's first-struck hourglass), so no JS branching per target.
- Board-anchored scenes that name ranks use HOME / EDGE / ROW / TS_ROW / GM_Y constants built on `--fx-side`, and move their marks off the cast banner where the rule does not pin them to the far ranks.

## REQUESTS

- Integrator (evidence budget): the evidence folder is over the 25 MB line (42 MB when round 2 started, other slices included). TC-god deleted its own `vs/` stacks (2.1 MB) this round and keeps round 2 strips at 6 x 140px; TC-god now holds about 5 MB.

- Integrator: move `docs/polish-pass/evidence/TC-god/check-god-weight.ts` to `scripts/check-god-weight.ts` and add `"test:god-weight": "tsx scripts/check-god-weight.ts"` to package.json (slice L owns package.json). It needs the relative imports changed from `../../../../src/...` to `../src/...`.
- Slice J (UseSpectacle / cast banner): the shared cast banner and frame pulse hold the board for about 2.4s whatever the card's tier, so a tier 4 play still reads as long even though its own scene now ends at about 1.9s (F227). Consider scaling the banner hold with tier.
- Slice J / B (TC0-a): `mass_mind_control`'s marks and `sealed_ramparts`' per-rook leash land on the target squares only when Board mounts target renders for them; in the strips the lead carries the rule on its own, which is the fallback.
- Slice J (cast banner placement): the card-name banner sits across ranks 7 and 8 from the viewer's side for the whole play, which is exactly where most hexes land (the opponent's back rank and the pieces in front of it). walnut_court's bench, chisel_curse's target on d7 and sacked_capital's capital rank are partly under it in the strips. The draft scenes moved their card rows two ranks in to stay clear of it; the rank-true scenes cannot move. A banner that sits on the caster's side, or fades after the tell, would let these read.
- Board (slice J): peace_of_the_grave needs the caster's king square (or a zone for the king's neighbours) passed to the play to draw its "no piece next to your king" rule truthfully.
