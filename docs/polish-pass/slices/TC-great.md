## Round 2 (tier 6 and 5)

Every tier 5 and 6 card in greatPlays now plays its own scene; no card at tier 5 or above rides a shared template. 85 cards moved in this round: all 28 live tier 6 cards, all 33 live tier 5 cards, and the 24 retired tier 6 and 5 cards. Passive cards have no cast square, so their scenes place the rule on absolute board squares through a new side-aware `sq(col, rank)` helper (rank 0 the caster's back rank); cards played on a square keep `cell` and `AIMED`. New shared kit, all transform and opacity: `Ray` (a move drawn, or drawn and cut back to what the rule allows), `Pips`, `Walnut`, `Cuff`, `Hourglass`, `IceBlock`, `Strike`, `TierCard`, `THEIR_ROW` / `OUR_ROW` (card rows clear of the cast banner). No new CSS: the round-1 grp-c-* verbs cover it.

Cleanup (0aac41b): the 62 flourish blocks whose cards now have scenes, and the SiegeRoll, WarBanner and Grove templates (no card left on them), are deleted, 1,630 lines. The ten remaining templates carry only the 18 cards of tier 4 and below.
