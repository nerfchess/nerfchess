## How the rule scenes work

A rule scene draws the pieces and squares the card's rule touches (the rank it opens, the move it grants, the capture or move it forbids, how many turns) plus the object its name is about (the changeling's fae mask and cradle, the watermelon rind, the drawbridge, the toll gate bar, the clapperboard of Cold Open, the calendar pages of Lost Weekend). There is no shared wash, rail, spectacle, shock ring or quake in any of them; the only accents left are a small glint or a dust mote where something lands. Scenes are registered with `S(Scene, config)` (the config objects, anchors and sound keys are unchanged, so PLUGIN_IDS, sound coverage and the anchor rule are untouched).

Two coordinate frames: board rules draw inside `<BoardFrame>` with `sq(file, rank)`, `band`, `area` and `lineTop`, ranks counted from the caster through `--fx-side`; piece rules draw on the 14-cell stage around the cast square with `cell(dx, dy)`, dy forward (away from the caster) through `--fx-side`. Every layer box is one square, so the new tracks move in whole squares: `bsp-r-move` (a move, `--mx` files and `--my` ranks forward), `bsp-r-hop` (a leap over an arc), `bsp-r-balk` (a move that the rule refuses: starts, stops short, is thrown back), `bsp-r-gone` (a piece removed or a charge spent), `bsp-r-strain` (a held piece), `bsp-r-fall` (Comet Shard's fall) and `bsp-r-hold` (a marked square or a standing piece). All animate transform and opacity only, end at opacity 0, are one-shots with durations times `--fx-dur`, use `--ease-out` / `--ease-io`, and are parked by the module's existing `html[data-anim="off"]` rule. Every beat offset goes through `dm(delayMs, off)`, which scales it by `--fx-dur`. No shadow, glow, filter or blur; each card keeps its existing three-colour palette (Watermelon Rind borrows three hexes already in the module: rind green, warm white, flesh red). The target and entrance roles keep each card's palette, device and face icon through `RuleCut`. Tier 1 scenes are short: about 0.8s of beats, visual end about 2.3s including the shared cast banner.
