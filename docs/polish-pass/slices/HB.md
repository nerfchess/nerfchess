## PLAN

### What the owner asked

"The bots are on purpose. It's to make my website look better. So don't touch that for now and also just improve the bots and stuff." Improve means: the house bots become better opponents and better company. They play at the strength their rating claims, use cards and drafts sensibly and with variety, manage their clock, resign and answer draws the way a real player at their level would, never stall or hang a game, and degrade gracefully when the remote engine is slow or down.

### Hard limits (binding on every track)

1. Unchanged: the personas (names, bios, avatars, tiers), roster size (900), the presence and active windows, `houseOnlineCount` and the public online figure, lobby presence, the TV and lobby filler (count, cadence, pacing, pools, pairing). F114 stays DROPPED.
2. No bot flag, eval, seed or trace in any client payload or page. New server-only fields (eval history, snap flag, breaker state) never go into a broadcast frame or a public endpoint. Bot resigns, draw answers and rematch answers use exactly the frames a human's would, with human-like delays.
3. Filler pacing is frozen: `houseThinkMs` with `thinkMultiplier > 1` must return exactly what it returns today for the same RNG draws. Filler may play better moves and use cards better, since that is bot quality, but its pacing, pools, pairing and counts do not move.
4. HB edits only HB files. `worker.ts`, `src/engine/game.ts`, `board.ts`, `replay.ts`, `moveSafety.ts` and the other rules files are read-only; every change there is a REQUEST below.
5. Production hosts (the OCI engine box, the arena host, the public site) get no load and no writes. Everything is measured locally.

### How the three scouts were merged

Three scouts reported on 2026-09-23: playing strength and search (S1), behaviour and feel (S2), reliability and ops (S3). They agree on five gaps, and those are ranked highest: the beginner tiers give their king away, the clock cliff, persona-less remote play, no resign or draw, and endgames thrown away. Their numbers come from scratchpad probes on a loaded box (load average 4 to 9 on 4 cores) and from models. Every track re-measures its "before" with its own committed script on the unmodified code first, records the commit hash in the evidence file, and quotes that number rather than the scout's. Where a claim was read from the code and never measured, the table says so.

### Cross-track contracts (additive only, so every caller keeps compiling)

- King-capture scores become `±(KING_CAPTURE_SCORE - ply)` with `export const KING_CAPTURE_SCORE = 100000` and `export function isKingCaptureScore(cp: number): boolean`. Magnitudes stay at or above 90000, the threshold `EvalBar.tsx:290-330` reads.
- `pickAIMove`, `analyzeBoard` and `aiBudgetMs` keep their signatures. `LEVELS` stays as it is for the client.

HB1 in `src/lib/server/bots.ts`:

- `pickHouseMove(game, skill, random, remainingClockMs?, ceilingMs?, profile?, persona?, opts?: HouseMoveOptions)` with `HouseMoveOptions = { varietySeed?: number; incrementSec?: number; stats?: SearchStats }`.
- `houseMoveBudgetMs(budgetMs, remainingClockMs?, ceilingMs?, incrementSec?)`.
- `houseThinkMs(random, myClockMs, timeSec, thinkMultiplier = 1, tempo = 1, opts?: HouseThinkOptions)` with `HouseThinkOptions = { incrementSec?: number; expectedSearchMs?: number; ownMoveIndex?: number; kingSafeMoves?: number; capturesAvailable?: number }`. With `thinkMultiplier > 1` the result is identical to today's.
- `houseExpectedSearchMs(profileBudgetMs, remainingClockMs, remote, incrementSec?)` and `houseEngineTimeoutMs(budgetMs)`.
