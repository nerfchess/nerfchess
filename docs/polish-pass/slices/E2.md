## SECURITY (review round 1)

- SECURITY, link-preview edge-cache poisoning (reviewer blocking finding on 49b591a): FIXED. `profileImage`, `clubImage`, `tournamentImage` and `inviteImage` in `src/lib/og/routes.tsx` built their `caches.default` key from the raw, Next-decoded route param, and `ogResponse` turned it into a URL, so `/tournaments/..%2Fgame%2FABCDE/opengraph-image/...` could store a "not found" card under `v1/game/ABCDE` (a finished game's month-long key), `%2e%2e/puzzles/x` under a puzzle's key, and `abc%23x` under a real player's or club's key. Fix, two layers: (1) each of the four now reads its record first and keys only on the record that was found (`p.username`, `c.slug`, `t.id`, and `inv.code` after `INVITE_CODE_RE`); a miss renders its "not found" or generic card with no key, so it is never pinned (this also closes the nit that a profile, club or tournament shared right after creation previewed as "not found" for an hour). `gameImage` now keys on the archive's validated `g.id` rather than the param. (2) `ogCacheRequest(key)` in `src/lib/og/render.ts` is the only place a key becomes a URL: every `/` segment is percent-encoded (so `#`, `?`, `\` and `%2e` stay inside their segment), an empty, `.` or `..` segment refuses the key, and the parsed URL must equal the written one; a refused key means no edge cache, never a wrong one. Regression: section 6 of `scripts/test-og-render.ts` installs a recording fake `caches.default`, calls every data-backed image with hostile params (`../game/ABCDE`, `..%2Fgame%2FABCDE`, `abc#x`, `abc?x`, `a/b`, `..\game\ABCDE`, `%2e%2e/puzzles/x`, ` ABCDE`) and with unknown well-formed ones, and asserts no key is touched; it also asserts `ogCacheRequest` refuses dot and empty segments and keeps every key under its own namespace. Before the fix: `og-cache-key-before.txt`, 101/147 (46 failures, e.g. `profile("../game/ABCDE")` touched `v1/game/abcde`, `tournament("%2e%2e/puzzles/x")` touched `v1/puzzles/x`). After: `og-cache-key-after.txt`, 147/147. Edge-cache keys under `v1/` for puzzles and codex cards are unchanged for plain ids (they were already built from found records).
