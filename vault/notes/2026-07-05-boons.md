# Boons for Nerf mode (punch list 19-20)

Date: 2026-07-05
Branch: claude/overnight-punchlist

## What changed

- Nerf mode now drafts from a dedicated boon pool instead of the full buff
  library. A boon is any nerf-relief card (category "nerf") plus a curated set
  of light general cards flagged `boon: true` in the library. Buff mode still
  excludes category "nerf" entirely; legacy merged games keep the full pool.
- Nerf mode draft cadence changed from every 10 own moves to every 6
  (NERF_MODE_CADENCE in src/engine/draft.ts). Worker and both clients derive
  the cadence from that constant via enableDraftMode; UI copy on the game,
  online, and friend pages now says "every six moves".
- Held boons now show in the corner: the player's own PlayerNerfCard (the
  rail card that carries the nerf) lists active boons with name, live status,
  and tier, in both bot games and online games. Mobile keeps the Boons drawer.

## Engine hooks added

- `Buff.boon?: boolean` flag plus `isBoon()` in src/engine/buff.ts. No new
  effect kinds were needed: all softening cards run on the existing
  `nerf_suspended` ActiveEffect, the `nerfRemoved` flag (`removeMyNerf`), and
  existing draft flags (prepThree, bankBonus, skips, extraMoves).

## The boon pool (implemented cards; * = new this change)

Tier 1 (3 relief / 3 general):
- Reprieve: suspend nerf 2 turns (relief)
- Deep Breath*: free action, ignore your nerf for one move (relief)
- Small Mercies*: next 2 enemy captures each suspend your nerf 1 turn (relief)
- Pawn Shield, Peek, Quick Glance (general)

Tier 2 (3 relief / 2 general):
- Slack in the Chain*: suspend 3 turns (relief)
- Held Breath*: free action, suspend 2 turns on demand (relief)
- Hunter's Relief*: your next 2 captures each suspend your nerf 1 turn (relief)
- Reinforce, Draft Insight (general)

Tier 3 (3 relief / 2 general):
- Respite: suspend 5 turns (relief)
- Timely Lull*: free action, suspend 3 turns on demand (relief)
- Underdog's Grit*: nerf suspended while down on pieces (relief)
- Extra Move, Bunker (general)

Tier 4 (2 relief / 2 general):
- Grace Period: suspend 4 turns (relief)
- Adrenaline*: nerf suspended whenever your king is in check (relief)
- Snap Freeze, Recast (general)

Tier 5 (2 relief / 2 general):
- Long Leash: suspend 7 turns (relief)
- Parole*: nerf removed for good after your next 10 turns (relief)
- Total Freeze, Iron Reign (general)

Tier 6 (3 relief / 2 general):
- Nerf Breaker: remove your nerf (relief)
- Warden's Bribe*: free action, suspend 6 turns on demand (relief)
- Iron Will*: every enemy capture suspends your nerf 2 turns (relief)
- Mass Resurrect, Sanctuary Zone (general)

Tier 7 (2 relief / 2 general):
- Sabbatical: suspend 10 turns (relief)
- Full Pardon*: remove your nerf and take an extra move (relief)
- Fortress Realm, Aegis (general)

Tier 8 (2 relief / 2 general):
- Unshackled Wrath*: remove your nerf, opponent skips a turn (relief)
- Transcendence*: remove your nerf, next draft shows 3 cards a tier higher (relief)
- Checkmate Denial, Absolute Aegis (general)

## Composition

- 37 implemented boons total, 20 nerf-relief.
- Nerf-relief share: 54.1% (target 50-60%).
- Every tier has 4 or more implemented boons; unimplemented stubs
  (Loosen the Leash, Piece Parole, Half Measure, Rehab, Nerf Reversal) stay
  cataloged but never draft.
